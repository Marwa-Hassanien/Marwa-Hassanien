My Story

I’m an Electrical BIM Technical Office Engineer with a B.Sc. in Electrical Power and Machines Engineering from Benha University (2024). I design power, lighting and low-current systems, build BIM models in Revit MEP, run cable sizing, voltage drop and load calculations, and coordinate with architectural, mechanical and structural disciplines. I’m especially interested in lighting design and construction, and I keep learning through courses and hands-on training.
